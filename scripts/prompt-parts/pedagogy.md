PEDAGOGY

- Prompts, explanations, context are in ENGLISH, simple and friendly.
- context: 120–250 words of markdown: the rule or situation, 4–8 example sentences with English glosses, 1–2 "watch out" notes.
- keyItems: 10–20 words/chunks the topic is built on; support = English gloss; note optional (gender, register, irregularity).
- Every exercise has an explanation of 1–2 sentences that states the rule or reason. Never just repeat the answer.
- The learner PRODUCES language. The most valuable exercises make the learner compose: build_phrase (a whole sentence from a situation or English meaning), question_for_answer (write the question for a given answer) and translate_word toTarget (English → Portuguese, allow letters/typing). Mix for 40 exercises: 10–12 build_phrase, 8–10 question_for_answer, 4–6 translate_word (toTarget), 6–8 fill_gaps (prefer 2–3 gaps), 4–5 find_error, 3 about_me, at most 2 match_pairs/order_dialogue in total (number topics may use number_sequence instead). Vocabulary topics keep the same productive core: at most 6 translate_word and 2 match_pairs.
- difficulty: 1 = recognise / short form; 2 = full sentence with one decision; 3 = two decisions or a longer sentence (6+ words). Spread ≈ 20% / 45% / 35% (≈ 8 / 18 / 14 of 40). Most difficulty-3 items are build_phrase or question_for_answer.
- Distractors must be plausible near misses (same part of speech, similar form: falo/falas/fala, de/do/da, sou/estou). Never random unrelated words. Never a distractor that also yields a correct sentence in the situation described. If the prompt is what disambiguates (register, gender, figure, time of day), the prompt must say so in English.
- A distractor must never equal an answer token once you ignore case, trailing . ! ? and guillemets («») — e.g. answer ["Tens","um","gato?"] must not use "tens" or "Tens?" as a distractor.
- For about_me, do not let a hint duplicate a sampleAnswer word (same case/punctuation-insensitive comparison) — the tile bank shows every hint and every sampleAnswer word, so a duplicate would show the same tile twice.
- No two exercises in a topic with the same target sentence.
- European Portuguese often drops the subject pronoun. For build_phrase and question_for_answer, put the variant with/without eu/tu/nós in acceptedAnswers.
- Questions use European word order: question word + (é que) + verb — «Onde moras?», «Onde é que moras?», «A que horas é que te levantas?». Use both forms across the topic; when the answer uses one, put the other in acceptedAnswers. Never write or accept Brazilian order («Onde você mora?», «Que horas você acorda?»).
- question_for_answer must be answerable from the prompt alone: the prompt says WHAT to ask about and the register, so only one question fits — e.g. "Your friend answers: «Às sete.» Ask her (informal) what time she gets up." Never a prompt that only shows the answer when several questions would fit (Quando? / A que horas? / Onde?). Put every other reasonable question in acceptedAnswers.
- find_error: the wrong token must be a real grammar or vocabulary error (verb ending, gender, article, preposition/contraction, ser/estar, a Brazilian form). Never an error that is only a missing or wrong accent/cedilla (es/és, e/é, esta/está, nao/não) — the app ignores accents, so such an item tests nothing. The options must also differ from each other by more than accents.
- Contractions are preposition + article: «a + o = ao», «a + a = à», «de + o = do», «em + a = na», «por + o = pelo». «Vou ao cinema» is explained as «ir a» + «o cinema» → «ao» — never as "ir + o".
- Before an explanation or "watch out" note calls a form wrong, be sure it really is wrong in European Portuguese. Do not invent contrasts. «de manhã», «à tarde», «à noite» and «de noite» are all correct; never teach "à noite, not de noite".
- Frequency adverbs: «sempre» normally goes AFTER the verb («Levanto-me sempre às sete», «Janto sempre em casa»); «nunca», «às vezes», «normalmente» usually go before it («Nunca bebo café»). Write «sempre» after the verb in answers, and never make the position of «sempre» the error in find_error.
